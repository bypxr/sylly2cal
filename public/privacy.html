Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

sylly2cal turns a course syllabus into calendar events. This page explains what happens to your data.

Files you upload

Your syllabus is read in memory on our server to find dates, then discarded. We do not save your files or the text in them, and we do not create accounts.

Calendar access

If you choose Google Calendar or Outlook, you sign in with your provider in a popup. The access token stays in your browser tab and is used only to add the events you reviewed. Our server never sees or stores it. It expires on its own, and you can remove access any time in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or analytics. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
